## Key Findings & Summary
- **Purpose:** Can LWIT differentiate TB-TTI from DTPI?
- **Design, participants & setting:** 10-bed palliative care unit; pts w/ skin change.
- Ethics were discussed – no more than minimal risk.
- Staff RN established competent staging/ID.
- WOC RN knew how to obtain images and enter data.
- LWIT, RN ID, temp variation & biomarkers were compared.
- Temp increase of area is DTPI; homogenous pattern is TB-TTI.
- No difference in size – difference in tissue type.

## Clinical Relevance
- Landmark article for this technology – integrating it into assessment and care planning can shape nursing.
- Also an important diagnostic tool for skin failure, w/ implications for palliative care.
- Ultimately benefits patients w/ understanding illness & choosing interventions.
