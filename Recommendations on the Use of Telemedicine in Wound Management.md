## Key Findings & Summary
- Telemed has advantages for patients, caregivers, and insurance.
- Can be used to develop prevention strategy.
- Methods – Delphi panel, ~~exp~~ EB analysis of telehealth lit – 315 publications 2000–2025.
- One study showed infrared thermography at home was good for preventing recurrence.
- Digital photography was valid, reliable, and feasible w/ audio/video & effective for monitoring.
- Improved clinician adherence to prevention – remote connection of professionals helped & reduced costs.
- Discussion of tele consult & tele expertise.
- Among statements w/ strong agreement was that tele is preferred when pt. is remote, immobile, & provides on the spot training for clinicians.
- Photography guidelines should be used.
- Long list of wounds can be managed from the start.

## Clinical Relevance
- Consider adoption for IP setting.
- Can better manage consult list as WOC w/ tele expertise & tele consult.
- Tele expertise can make it easier for clinical RN to manage time.
- Tele expertise can be improved w/ wound PCT or LPN.
- Create policy for tele consult / tele expertise by pt. condition & wound type.
- Create business to assist w/ prevention via tele consult?

## Direct Quotes & Citations
> "The remote involvement of the expert allows for a rapid diagnosis, a timely decision on the right care to be provided, at the right time, and in the most appropriate setting (e.g., community/home/hospital), considering the respective skills of the caregiver and the patient's need." (p. 568)
